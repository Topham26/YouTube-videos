## Lyrics

**Verse 1**
Holy, holy, holy — Lord God Almighty,
Early in the morning our song shall rise to Thee.
Holy, holy, holy — none in the heavens like Thee,
Feared in the assembly of the holy ones on high.

**Verse 2**
Holy, holy, holy — the sons of God adore Thee,
Casting down their crowns before the fiery throne.
Watchers and the seraphs bow within Thy council,
Who among the mighty ones can stand before Thee, Lord?

*(Psalm 89:6–7; 1 Kings 22:19; Isaiah 6)*

**Chorus (new — not in the original hymn)**
You divided up the nations,
Set their bounds by heaven's sons —
But Jacob is Your portion,
Israel the one You won.
Rise, O God, and judge the rulers,
Take the nations for Your own —
Every knee in heaven's council
Bends before Your throne alone.

*(Deut 32:8–9; Psalm 82:1, 8)*

**Verse 3**
Holy, holy, holy — though rebel powers defied Thee,
Though the gods of nations led the peoples far astray,
Thou alone art faithful — the Judge of all the council,
"You shall die like mortals, and fall like any prince."

*(Psalm 82:2–7)*

**Verse 4**
Holy, holy, holy — Lord God Almighty,
All Thy works shall praise Thy name in earth and sky and sea.
Holy, holy, holy — the Name above the elim,
God enthroned in glory, and reigning over all.

**Bridge (build)**
God stands in the assembly — He holds court among the gods.

Arise, O God! Judge the earth!
For all the nations belong to You.
*(repeat, building)*

**Final Chorus** — as chorus, then tag:
Every knee in heaven's council

## Arrangement Guide (Modern Worship / Cinematic)

- **Key:** D major (original is often in Eb; D sits well for congregational range and open guitar voicings). Capo 2 shapes in C if preferred.
- **Tempo:** 72 bpm, 4/4. The original is in a stately 4/4 already, so it converts cleanly.
- **Form:** Intro (ambient pad + piano motif) → V1 (piano + light pad) → V2 (add acoustic, soft kick on 1 and 3) → Chorus (full band in) → V3 (pull back to half instrumentation) → Chorus → Bridge (build from near-silence to full wall of sound, add choir/gang vocals on "Arise, O God") → Final Chorus (double chorus, tag ending) → Outro (return to the opening piano motif, let it decay).
- **Harmonic tweak for a modern feel:** where the hymn resolves I–IV–I, substitute I–vi–IV–V in the chorus (D–Bm–G–A), and land the bridge on a suspended IV (Gsus2) before the final chorus drop.
- **Production touches:** side-chained pad swells, a low synth drone under the bridge (fits the throne-room gravity), sparse electric guitar with dotted-eighth delay, and stacked BGVs in octaves on the final chorus.
