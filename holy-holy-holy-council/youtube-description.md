## Description

**Holy, Holy, Holy (The Council of the Holy Ones)**

A re-imagining of Reginald Heber's 1826 hymn "Holy, Holy, Holy," reframed around
the biblical *divine council* — the assembly of heavenly beings over which God
presides as unrivaled King and Judge. New verses and a new chorus set Heber's
adoration inside the throne-room of Psalm 82, Deuteronomy 32, and Isaiah 6.

The chorus draws on **Deuteronomy 32:8–9**, where God "divided up the nations"
and "set their bounds" — and takes Israel as His own portion. The bridge and
third verse move into **Psalm 82**, where "God stands in the assembly; He holds
court among the gods," pronounces judgment on the rebel powers who ruled the
nations unjustly — "You shall die like mortals, and fall like any prince" — and
is called to "arise and judge the earth, for all the nations belong to You."

**Key texts**
• Psalm 82:1, 6–8 — God judges in the divine assembly
• Deuteronomy 32:8–9 — the nations apportioned; Israel as God's portion
• Psalm 89:5–7 — none in the heavens compares; God feared in the council of the holy ones
• 1 Kings 22:19–23 — the LORD enthroned, all the host of heaven standing by
• Isaiah 6:1–3 — the thrice-holy God, high and lifted up

**A note for those who check their Bibles:**
The chorus follows the reading of **Deuteronomy 32:8** attested in the Dead Sea
Scrolls (4QDeut^j) and the Septuagint — "according to the number of the *sons of
God* / *angels of God*" — rather than the Masoretic Text's "sons of Israel." Many
modern translations (ESV, NRSV) adopt this older reading. It's the interpretive
key to the whole song.
